## What's covered

How to map vendors to the problems they actually solve, translate marketing claims into verifiable specifications, price total cost of ownership, and avoid lock-in through portability and contract terms. Neutral evaluation framework; no vendor is endorsed and none is disparaged — the course confers no certificate.
