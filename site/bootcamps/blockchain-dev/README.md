## What's covered

Hands-on foundations of blockchain systems: EVM execution and Solidity, wallets and key custody, consensus models, Bitcoin/Monero/Stellar ledger concepts, and ISO 20022 payment messaging. Vendor-neutral coverage of public concepts only; the course confers no certificate and endorses no chain, token, or product.
