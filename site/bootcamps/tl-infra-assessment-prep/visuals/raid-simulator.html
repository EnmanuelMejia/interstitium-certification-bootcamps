Rules of thumb: RAID 0 = speed, zero redundancy · RAID 1/10 = mirroring, survives one failure per pair · RAID 5 = one parity disk, survives any single failure · RAID 6 = double parity, survives any two · a second failure during a RAID 5 rebuild = data loss (the classic exam trap).
