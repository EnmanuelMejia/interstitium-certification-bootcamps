3 broadcast domainsone per VLAN — each spans both switches over the trunk
What you're seeing: a VLAN is a broadcast domain — broadcasts stay inside it. The 802.1Q trunk carries all three VLANs between switches by tagging each frame (4 extra bytes). Kill the trunk and each VLAN's broadcast domain splits in two — SRV-01 can no longer reach SRV-02 even though they're on the same VLAN. A router (or layer-3 switch) doing inter-VLAN routing is the only way traffic crosses VLANs.
